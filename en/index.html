About

Code, radio waves, and a little Gensokyo.

I'm Pleniluner / 清月.

You can also call me Elysia.

Where the names come from

Pleniluner comes from Plenilune Annals (Yingyue Jinian), and is the name I use in more formal settings. I chose it because Plenilune Annals is the earliest documented Chinese Touhou fan work released in Japan. That history means a great deal to me.

Elysia comes from Elysia's Heart, and is a more familiar name. I’ve loved this song for years. It feels light, clear and vivid, and I hope to carry that spirit with me, moving forward with courage and independence.

I write C/C++ for systems and performance, Kotlin for Android applications, and Python and MATLAB for computation, images, and signals. My interests also reach into compilers, processor design, machine learning, and computer graphics.

I like following a program down to its instructions, working through a mathematical problem, and finding something recognizable in a noisy recording.

I'm also an architecture student, amateur radio operator BG7LBJ, stargazer, and occasional Minecraft architect. Somewhere between the code and the drawings, there is usually a Touhou soundtrack playing.

On my workbench

Astronomy on Android

Developing an application for celestial-event tracking with Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, and the Android SDK.

I'm interested in interfaces that make astronomical information useful at a glance, along with the animation, gestures, caching, and performance work that makes an application comfortable to use.

Radio, signals & connected devices

Exploring SDR, spectrum analysis, protocol decoding, and digital communications, alongside an IoT project combining radio communication and deep learning.

My radio experiments give me a practical setting for sampling theory, filters, modulation, and noise analysis.

Images, audio & scientific data

Working with open astronomical datasets and exploring image calibration, stacking, and post-processing.

On the audio side, my interests include digital filtering, codecs, mobile audio processing, and room correction. I also explore WebRTC for real-time audio, video, and data communication.

Algorithms & mathematical problems

Practicing ACM-style problem solving, implementing data structures, and working through graph algorithms, dynamic programming, and number-theoretic problems.

I also enjoy recreational mathematics and AMC problems for their own sake.

Architecture & imagined places

Developing ideas around Neo-Chinese architecture, Chinese ink-wash painting, and contemporary spatial design.

Some ideas become sketches and models; others become buildings in Minecraft.

Assembly, hardware & parallel programming

x86 / x86-64 assembly · MIPS assembly · Verilog · VHDL · CUDA C++ · ISPC · POSIX threads · FPGA

My systems work includes memory management, interrupts, processes, scheduling, synchronization, and file-system interfaces. I use assembly and debugging tools to inspect calling conventions, stack frames, memory layout, and executable behavior.

In digital hardware, I work with combinational and sequential logic, processor pipelines, forwarding, stalls, exceptions, and memory-mapped I/O.

For parallel programs, I examine thread synchronization, vectorization, cache behavior, GPU memory access, and the overheads that limit speedup.

Compilers & software construction

I work with lexical analysis, parsing, abstract syntax trees, symbol tables, type checking, intermediate representations, control-flow analysis, and register allocation.

My compiler toolkit includes Flex / JFlex and YACC / BYACC/J.

In C++, I use ownership, RAII, move semantics, templates, and the STL. I organize software around clear interfaces, manageable dependencies, reproducible builds, and tests that exercise meaningful behavior.

Applications, interfaces & networks

Android SDK · Jetpack Compose · Qt / Qt Widgets · Java Swing Django · SQLite · Scrapy · Lucene · WebRTC

I build event-driven applications with persistent state, background work, and network communication.

This includes Android lifecycle behavior, Qt signals and slots, database access, and responsive interfaces. With sockets, I account for message framing, partial reads, timeouts, and concurrent connections.

My networking background covers TCP/IP, routing, congestion, DNS, HTTP, authentication, cryptographic primitives, and transport security.

Algorithms, machine learning & search

I work with trees, heaps, hashing, string algorithms, graphs, dynamic programming, network flow, and heuristic search. I analyze complexity and choose data structures around the workload.

For machine learning, I can implement classical models and neural networks, derive and check gradients, and evaluate results using appropriate data splits and metrics. My background includes probabilistic models, classification, clustering, CNNs, recurrent networks, and attention.

For search, I work with tokenization, inverted indexes, TF–IDF, BM25, and PageRank. I pay attention to data leakage, error analysis, and reproducibility.

Graphics, images & signals

My graphics work includes geometric transformations, camera models, ray tracing, path tracing, and spatial acceleration structures.

For images and signals, I work with filtering, resampling, feature extraction, convolution, Fourier analysis, FFTs, FIR/IIR filters, and time-frequency analysis.

I use these ideas in astronomical imaging, audio experiments, and radio. Aliasing, spectral leakage, numerical error, and filter response matter as much as the final picture or waveform.
